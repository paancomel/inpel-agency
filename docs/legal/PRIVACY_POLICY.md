- **INPELER**, the portal used by authorised university representatives to publish institutional and programme information;
- **INPOLOR**, the university review and community service;

It explains what we collect, where it comes from, why we use it, who receives it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights available under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (**PDPA**), as amended.

## 2. Important summary

- We use parent and student data to build education profiles and generate university, programme and career recommendations.
- The matching process involves automated profiling. It is advisory only and does not decide admission, scholarships or employment.
- With the required consent, relevant data **will be shared with selected partner universities** for enquiries, enrolment support, marketing and analytics.
- Partner-university marketing and non-essential advertising tracking are optional and require separate consent.
- Meta/Facebook and TikTok advertising tools must remain disabled until advertising consent is given.
- INPEL accepts students aged 15 to 17 only with recorded parent or legal guardian consent; it does not accept users under 15.
- INPOLOR is for people aged 18 or older only; we collect a date of birth and record the age declaration for this gate.
- You may ask to access, correct, port or delete data, withdraw consent, object to direct marketing, or raise a complaint.

## 3. Personal data we collect

We collect only data reasonably needed for the stated purposes. Whether a field is mandatory or optional is shown in the form or consent screen.

### 3.1 Parent and family data

The INPEL parent flow currently collects or creates:

- parent email address;
- preferred Malaysian study location or an indication that the student is open to any location;
- monthly household income band;
- preferred type of campus environment;
- primary campus concern, such as academic quality, safety, mental health support or industry connections;
- preferred education outcome, such as employment, professional network, leadership development or international opportunities;
- the parent's view of the student's level of independence and support needs;
- family session and invitation identifiers;
- invitation, creation, completion and parent-notification timestamps and status; and
- consent and communication records.

Household income and family preferences are optional for general browsing but may be required to generate cost-sensitive matching. If they are not provided, some matches or affordability features may be unavailable or less accurate.

### 3.2 Student account and authentication data

We may collect or receive:

- student email address and user identifier;
- authentication method, such as password, Google or Facebook;
- authentication, confirmation and session timestamps;
- profile role and account status;
- information returned by an authentication provider according to the permission screen; and
- security and account recovery information.

Passwords are handled by the authentication service and are not stored in the Platform's browser drafts. We do not receive a Google or Facebook password.

### 3.3 Academic and assessment data

The INPEL assessment currently collects or creates:

- up to 20 SPM subjects and the grade for each subject;
- answers on a five-point scale to 16 personality and work-style questions;
- five self-rated psychometric scores: analytical thinking, creative expression, people energy, hands-on learning and initiative;
- six learning and campus preference choices covering social setting, campus environment, teamwork, routine, learning mode and local or global outlook;
- calculated career clusters and strength profiles;
- assessment progress, draft, submission and completion timestamps;
- matched universities and programmes, match scores and reasons;
- cost, return-on-investment, scholarship and career pathway outputs; and
- selected report tier and payment status.

These assessments are not clinical or medical tests. We nevertheless treat raw answers, profiles, household income and academic records as high-sensitivity data and apply stronger controls to them.

### 3.4 University representative and institutional data

INPELER may collect or create:

- representative email, user ID, authentication data and authorised role;
- institution name, city or state, address and website;
- public contact email and telephone number;
- logo and campus image URLs and image categories;
- tuition fees, living costs and acceptance rate;
- facilities and student services;
- programme name, MQA code, tuition fee, duration, study mode, overview and entry requirements;
- accuracy attestation, publication status and timestamps; and
- technical records connected with publication.

Most institution and programme information is intended for public display. A representative must not submit a personal telephone number, image or other personal data without authority.

### 3.5 Reviews and community data

INPOLOR may collect or create:

- authentication email and user ID for signed-in users;
- date of birth, age-check result, age declaration and the time of declaration;
- course or major and year of study;
- one-to-five-star rating;
- written positive points, negative points and candid experience text;
- selected campus or experience tags;
- choice to post anonymously or as a verified student;
- comments, likes, author label, timestamps and moderation information; and
- for the quick-review flow, course, year and rating without identity details.

Review content is public. An anonymous choice hides the author's identity from public display and, where designed, removes the user ID and email from the review record. It cannot guarantee complete technical anonymity because security logs, legal obligations or information within the review itself may identify a person.

### 3.6 Technical, usage and device data

When the Platform is used, we or our providers may process:

- IP address, approximate location derived from IP, browser, device and operating system;
- date, time, page, referral URL, language and interaction events;
- authentication, error, security and audit logs;
- cookie, local-storage, session and consent identifiers;
- invitation and campaign parameters; and
- crash, performance and fraud-prevention signals.

The current applications also use browser local storage for family sessions, assessment drafts, institutional drafts and locally saved reviews. A short-lived authentication redirect draft is designed to expire after 24 hours and excludes passwords and provider tokens.

### 3.7 Advertising and campaign data

If advertising tools are active and you consent, Meta/Facebook, TikTok and similar providers may collect or receive:

- IP address and device or browser identifiers;
- advertising cookies and provider identifiers;
- referral, campaign and page-view information; and
- restricted events such as registration, assessment start or completion, university enquiry or report purchase.

We do **not** intentionally send advertising providers raw SPM grades, household income bands, personality answers, psychometric scores, assessment reports or review text.

### 3.8 Payment data

INPEL reports are intended to be one-time purchases in Malaysian Ringgit (RM), at launch prices of RM18, RM28 or RM48; no recurring subscription is intended. Where live payments are enabled, we may receive transaction identifier, report tier, amount, currency, payment status, time and limited billing information. Card numbers and security codes should be collected directly by Stripe or another clearly identified appointed payment provider and not stored by us.

## 6. Automated profiling and matching

The Platform uses submitted personal data to evaluate interests, academic fit, preferences, likely learning environment, cost fit and possible career direction. That is profiling.

The matching process may:

1. validate and structure parent and student answers;
2. calculate trait or career-cluster signals;
3. compare those signals with university, programme, fee, location and entry information;
4. calculate and rank match scores; and
5. generate reasons, comparisons, cost scenarios and report content.

The result is guidance only. It does not automatically accept or reject a university, scholarship, visa or job application, and we do not send a decision instruction to a university.

You may contact Admin@inpel.edu.my to request information about the main factors used, correct input data, object where the PDPA allows, request a fresh calculation, or seek reasonable human review. We may ask for information needed to verify identity and locate the relevant session.

We will not intentionally use sensitive assessment data to infer race, religion, health, disability or another protected characteristic for advertising or unlawful discrimination.

## 7. Consent for student data and minors

The age of majority in Malaysia is generally 18. INPEL accepts students aged 15 to 17 only. Before such a student submits personal, academic or assessment data, a parent or legal guardian must give recorded consent. We do not knowingly accept INPEL users under 15. INPOLOR is only for users aged 18 or older; the onboarding flow collects date of birth, validates the age on our server and records the user's age declaration.

Our INPEL consent flow is:

- the parent or guardian starts or verifies the family session and signs in or otherwise completes the parent-led flow;
- the parent or guardian receives this notice, confirms parental responsibility or lawful authority, and actively confirms consent;
- we retain the consent choice, time, family-session identifier and parent account identifier where available; and
- the student may continue only through the linked invitation; and
- separate choices are recorded for profiling, university sharing, partner marketing and advertising tracking.

The consent record supports auditability; it does not itself prove a person's identity or parental authority. We may request verification and may prevent submission or disclosure until it is complete. A parent or guardian may exercise a minor's rights, subject to identity and authority checks. When the student turns 18, the student may confirm, change or withdraw earlier choices.

Contact Admin@inpel.edu.my if you believe a person under 15 used INPEL or a person under 18 used INPOLOR.

## 8. Disclosure to partner universities

When the student or parent actively opts to share, requests contact, submits an enquiry or applies, the full INPEL profile **will be disclosed to the selected or matched partner universities**. The sharing screen must obtain a separate, unticked affirmative choice before transmission.

The disclosure includes the student's and parent's contact information, SPM results, raw and derived assessment information (including personality and psychometric answers or scores), stated interests, location preference, household income band or budget signal, family preferences, recommended programme, match score, report outputs and enquiry history. The exact fields must be shown at the point of sharing.

Partner universities may use the information to:

- respond to enquiries and provide course information;
- assess admission, intake or scholarship suitability;
- support application and enrolment;
- contact the student or parent about the enquiry;
- with separate marketing consent, promote relevant courses, events, intakes and scholarships; and
- measure enquiries, conversion, enrolment and matching quality.

Where practical, the consent screen will identify the universities by name or link to a current list. Once a university receives the data, it will normally be an independent data controller and its privacy notice will apply. You should contact both us and the university if you want data already shared to be corrected, deleted or no longer used for marketing.

We may share aggregated or properly de-identified trends with universities for analytics and service improvement where individuals are not reasonably identifiable.

## 10. Cookies, pixels and local storage

### 10.1 Categories

| Category | What it does | Choice |

| Strictly necessary | Security, authentication, invitation routing, session continuity, saved consent and essential drafts | Required for requested functions |
| Preferences | Remembers interface and non-essential user choices | Optional unless needed for a requested setting |

### 10.2 Google Analytics, Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel

After the relevant optional consent, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel may recognise a browser or device, record visits and restricted actions, and measure visits, reliability and campaigns. Meta and TikTok may match those events with an account held by the provider, build audiences and display targeted or retargeted advertising on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or affiliated services. Google, Meta and TikTok may act as independent controllers for parts of their own processing under their privacy terms.

Rejecting analytics or advertising cookies will not block the core assessment or portal. You can withdraw consent at any time through **Cookie Settings**. The withdrawal stops future loading on that browser after the setting is applied but cannot reverse earlier provider processing.

### 10.3 Browser storage

The Platform uses local storage to keep certain sessions and drafts on the device. Unlike a traditional cookie, local storage is not automatically sent with each web request, but anyone with access to the browser profile may be able to access it. Do not use a shared or public device for sensitive assessments. Clear saved data through the Platform where offered or through browser settings.

## 11. Direct marketing

We or a partner university will send direct marketing only with the required consent or where otherwise permitted by law. Marketing may be tailored using selected courses, matches, location, interaction or campaign information, but raw personality, psychometric, income and SPM data will not be placed into advertising-platform event labels.

You may opt out at any time by:

- using the unsubscribe link in an email;
- replying **STOP** where that instruction is offered;
- changing communication or cookie settings; or
- emailing Admin@inpel.edu.my with the subject **Stop Marketing**.

We may retain a minimal suppression record so the opt-out is respected. Service messages about accounts, security, an active enquiry, transaction or legal change are not marketing.

## 12. Cross-border transfers

Some cloud, authentication, support, analytics, advertising or university systems may process data outside Malaysia, including in countries whose laws differ from Malaysia's.

Before a cross-border transfer, we will identify the relevant class of recipient and purpose, use a transfer condition permitted by section 129 of the PDPA, obtain consent where that condition requires it, and take reasonable steps to ensure a level of protection consistent with the PDPA. Measures may include vendor due diligence, contractual safeguards, access controls, encryption and transfer records.

Contact Admin@inpel.edu.my for information about the relevant recipient locations or safeguards.

## 13. Retention and deletion schedule

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose, dispute handling, security and legal obligations. Our production retention schedule is intended to be:

| Record | Normal retention |
| --- | --- |
| Short-lived authentication redirect draft | Up to 24 hours |
| Incomplete assessment or invitation with no verified account activity | 90 days after last activity |
| Active account and completed assessment | Until the user requests deletion, or until we determine the record is no longer necessary for the service, security, dispute handling or a legal obligation |
| Recommendation and report | Until the user requests deletion, or until no longer necessary for the linked service; a de-identified statistical record may be kept longer |
| University enquiry and sharing record | Until the user requests deletion or the record is no longer necessary for the enquiry; the recipient university applies its own schedule |
| Marketing and advertising consent | Until withdrawal or 24 months after last marketing interaction; suppression records may be kept longer |
| Review, comment or like | Until deleted or account closure, subject to moderation, dispute and public-thread integrity needs |
| Institution profile and programme data | While published and for a reasonable archival or dispute period after withdrawal |
| Payment and accounting record | Seven years or another period required by Malaysian tax, accounting or anti-fraud law |
| Security and access logs | Normally 12 months; longer if needed for an incident, investigation or legal claim |
| Consent, rights request and legal compliance record | Up to seven years after the relevant relationship or request |

Browser-stored drafts may remain until the Platform or user clears them. Before production launch, automatic expiry must be applied to sensitive browser records so the implementation matches this schedule.

At the end of a retention period, data is securely deleted, destroyed or irreversibly de-identified.

## 14. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the data and risk. These should include encryption in transit, protected cloud storage, role-based access, least-privilege administration, logging, secure development, tested backups, staff confidentiality, vendor controls and incident response.

No online service is completely secure. Users should use a unique password, protect private links and avoid shared devices. Notify Admin@inpel.edu.my promptly if you suspect misuse.

Where a notifiable personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and affected individuals within the periods and in the manner required by the PDPA and applicable guidance.

## 15. Your rights and choices

Subject to the PDPA and lawful exceptions, you may:

- ask whether we process your personal data;
- request access to a copy;
- request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, misleading or outdated data;
- withdraw consent for future consent-based processing;
- ask us to stop processing likely to cause unwarranted damage or distress;
- require us to stop direct marketing;
- request technically feasible data portability to another controller;
- ask for deletion or irreversible de-identification; and
- complain to us or the Personal Data Protection Commissioner.

The PDPA does not create an unlimited right to erasure in every situation. We will nevertheless honour a deletion request where reasonably possible and will explain if data must be retained for a transaction, legal obligation, security, dispute, suppression list or another lawful reason. Withdrawing data needed for matching may require us to stop the assessment, report or sharing service.

### How to make a request

Email Admin@inpel.edu.my with the subject **Personal Data Request** and include:

- your name and account email;
- the family session, review or institution concerned, if known;
- the right you want to exercise; and
- enough detail to locate the data.

We may ask for reasonable proof of identity and, for a minor, proof of parental or guardian authority. An authorised representative must provide evidence of authority. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and may charge only a fee permitted by law.

For data already sent to a partner university or advertising provider, we will take reasonable steps within our control and tell you when a separate request to that recipient is also needed.

## 16. Consequences of not providing data

Public browsing does not require an assessment. However:

- without parent and student profile data, personalised matching cannot be generated;
- without an email or authentication method, we may not be able to save or restore an account across devices;
- without explicit profiling consent, we cannot process high-sensitivity assessment data for personalised matching;
- without university-sharing consent or an active sharing request, we will not send the profile to a university;
- without payment data, a paid report cannot be supplied; and
- without required institution information and attestation, a university profile cannot be published.

Optional marketing and advertising choices do not affect core access.
