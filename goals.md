# My Goals

## The Bigger Picture

I am building an education ecosystem designed to help school leavers make better decisions about what course and university they should choose.

The core problem I care about is this:

Young people are expected to make one of the biggest decisions of their early life at around 17 or 18 years old.

They need to decide:

- what to study,
- where to study,
- what career direction to take,
- and sometimes how much money their family should commit.

But many of them do not yet understand themselves well enough.

- They may not know what they are good at.
- They may not know what they actually enjoy.
- They may not understand what a course is really like.
- They may not understand what life at a particular university is actually like.

Parents also want to help, but they often make decisions with incomplete information.

Universities provide information about themselves, but naturally that information is usually presented from the university's own perspective.

So I want to reduce this information gap.

## What I Am Building

I currently see the solution as an ecosystem of three connected products.

### 1. University Reviews

A place where current students and alumni can share their real experiences.

The purpose is to help future students and parents understand what studying at a university or course is actually like from the perspective of people who experienced it.

Think of the basic idea as something similar to Glassdoor, but for universities and education.

### 2. University and Course Matching

A platform where students and parents provide relevant information about the student.

This may include things such as:

- academic performance,
- interests,
- preferences,
- budget,
- location,
- constraints,
- goals,
- and other relevant factors.

The system should then help narrow down and match the student with courses and universities that may suit them better.

The goal is not to tell someone:

> "This is definitely the perfect university for you."

The goal is to help them make a more informed decision.

### 3. University Admin Portal

A platform where universities can maintain accurate information about:

- courses,
- fees,
- requirements,
- facilities,
- locations,
- and other relevant information.

This gives the ecosystem better structured data to work with.

## The Bigger Mission

The mission is NOT simply:

> "Help people search for universities."

There are already many university directories.

The mission is:

> Help young people make better education decisions.

I want to reduce the chance that someone spends years studying something they later realize was never suitable for them.

I cannot guarantee that someone will make the perfect decision.

Life changes. People change. Interests change.

But I believe we can give people better information, better questions and better decision-making tools before they commit.

That is the problem I want to spend time understanding.

## My Build-in-Public Goal

At the same time, I want to build this project publicly through my personal brand.

I am not trying to become a generic startup content creator.

I am not trying to post:

> "Day 24 building my startup."

I want the public to follow my attempt to understand this problem.

The content should document things such as:

- assumptions I had that turned out to be wrong,
- problems I discover while building,
- things parents care about,
- things educators notice,
- things students struggle with,
- difficult decisions in the product,
- unanswered questions,
- surprising data,
- conversations that change my thinking,
- and experiments I run.

The product development itself becomes part of the story.

But the code is not the main story. The problem is the main story.

## Who I Want to Reach

My primary audience for my public content is:

- Parents
- Educators

Students are also relevant, but parents and educators are especially important because they have experience, opinions and influence around education decisions.

I want these people to see my work and think:

> "This person is genuinely trying to understand this problem."

Not:

> "This person is trying to sell me another education app."

## My Personal Brand Goal

I want to gradually become associated with one idea:

> Helping people make better education decisions.

When someone thinks about:

- choosing a university,
- choosing a course,
- understanding whether a course fits a student,
- student decision-making,
- parent concerns around higher education,

I eventually want my name and my work to be relevant to that conversation.

I am building credibility by doing the work publicly.

Not by pretending to already have all the answers.

It is acceptable for me to say:

> "I don't know yet."

In fact, documenting how my thinking changes is an important part of the brand.

## My Business Goal

The build-in-public effort should also help create demand for the product.

One concrete goal is to grow a relevant waitlist.

But I do NOT want every post to be an advertisement.

The preferred sequence is:

> Useful idea → interesting conversation → trust → curiosity about what I am building → relevant people join the waitlist.

The audience should first care about the problem. Then they can care about my solution.

Please remember this:

> I am not building in public because I want people to watch me code.
> I am building in public because I want people to follow me as I try to solve an important problem.

The story is not:

> "Hadi is building software."

The story is:

> "Can we help young people make better decisions before choosing what and where to study?"

The software is my attempt to answer that question.
